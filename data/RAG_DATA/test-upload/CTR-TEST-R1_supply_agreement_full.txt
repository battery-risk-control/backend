SYNTHETIC GRAPHITE SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This agreement is entered into between LG Energy Solution, Ltd.
("Buyer") and Penang Advanced Materials ("Seller").

Article 1
DEFINITIONS

1.01 "Material" means Seller's synthetic graphite meeting the
     specifications set out in Schedule A.
1.02 "Business Day" means a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a
     day on which banks are closed in Korea or Malaysia.
1.03 "Base Volume Commitment" has the meaning in Section 2.01.
1.04 "Force Majeure Event" has the meaning in Section 5.01.

Article 2
SUPPLY COMMITMENT

2.01 Seller shall supply a Base Volume Commitment of 6,000 MT per
     contract year, representing a 30 percent supply share.
2.02 Buyer shall issue purchase orders no later than 45 days prior
     to the requested delivery date.
2.03 If Seller's production capacity falls short of total demand,
     Seller shall allocate the Base Volume Commitment to Buyer in
     priority to supplies to other customers.

Article 3
PRICE AND PAYMENT

3.01 The unit price is USD 18.40 per metric ton, recalculated quarterly.
3.02 Payment is due within 30 days of receipt of shipping documents.
3.03 Delivery terms are CIF Busan under INCOTERMS 2020.

Article 4
QUALITY AND INSPECTION

4.01 Material shall meet a minimum purity of 99.9 percent.
4.02 Buyer shall inspect within 14 days of receipt.
4.03 Seller shall maintain IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 certification.

Article 5
FORCE MAJEURE

5.01 "Force Majeure Event" means any event beyond a party's reasonable
     control, including natural disaster, war, strike, government
     export restriction, port closure, or power interruption.
5.02 The affected party shall give written notice within 5 Business Days.
5.03 If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than 90 consecutive
     days, the other party may terminate this agreement.

Article 6
SUPPLY DISRUPTION AND ALTERNATIVE SOURCING

6.01 If Seller delays delivery by more than 21 days past the scheduled
     delivery date, Buyer may source the affected volume from a third party.
6.02 Excess costs incurred under Section 6.01 shall be borne by Seller.
