MASTER BATTERY CELL SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Battery Cell Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of May 07, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:
SELLER: LG Energy Solution, Ltd.
BUYER: Tesla, Inc.

ARTICLE 1. SUBJECT OF AGREEMENT
1.1 The Seller agrees to manufacture and supply to the Buyer High-Nickel Pouch Cells (the "Products") for use in the Buyer's electric vehicle (EV) manufacturing.

ARTICLE 2. VOLUME AND PRICING
2.1 Total Commitment: The Seller shall supply a total capacity of 69 GWh over the term of this Agreement.
2.2 The base price per kWh shall be USD 118, subject to quarterly adjustments based on the LME metal index.

ARTICLE 3. DELIVERY AND DELAY PENALTIES (LIQUIDATED DAMAGES)
3.1 Time is of the essence. The Seller must deliver the Products strictly in accordance with the Buyer's weekly delivery schedules.
3.2 Late Delivery Penalty: In the event of a delivery delay, the Seller shall pay to the Buyer a penalty of 0.2% of the delayed shipment value per day.
3.3 Line-Stop Charge: If the Seller's failure to deliver the Products on time causes a production halt at the Buyer's vehicle assembly plant (Line-Stop), the Seller shall be liable for a Line-Stop Charge of USD 10,000,000 for each full day of production stoppage, in addition to the late delivery penalty.

ARTICLE 4. FORCE MAJEURE AND EXCLUSIONS
4.1 Neither party shall be liable for delay due to true events of Force Majeure (acts of God, war, pandemic).
4.2 Shortages of raw materials or tier-2 supplier delays shall NOT be considered an event of Force Majeure.

ARTICLE 5. QUALITY WARRANTY
5.1 The Seller warrants that the Products will be free from defects in material and workmanship for a period of 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever occurs first.
